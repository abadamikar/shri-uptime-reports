3 · Average daily use by day of week

Each bar shows the average number of uses recorded at a single school on that day of the week. For example, the Monday bars add up the uses from every Monday at every school, and divide by the number of school-Mondays with data. The number of school-days behind each bar differs slightly (89 to 102) because schools were closed on some days for holidays, examinations and vacations, and these closures do not fall evenly across the week. Schools are closed on Sundays.
